---
name: Mobile polish guardrails
description: Durable UI rules for extending the OG Landmark Expo app without changing its product identity or creating invalid web markup.
---

Use the shared semantic tokens and reusable controls for new mobile UI, while preserving the current navy/gold identity, the official roof/house + OG mark in theme navy (#0B1F3A), and navigation. Keep the former three-pillar mark out of the product. Use a transparent symbol-only logo without the LANDMARK wordmark for app icons and compact headers. For native splash and app-opening loading screens, center the official mark inside two subtle rings with four polished markers on each, then show “OG Landmark” beneath it; keep the branded startup splash visible for at least 7 seconds, rotate the rings continuously in opposite directions (outer 2.8-second and inner 3.6-second rotations) unless reduced motion is enabled, and wait for normal route readiness before revealing the app. Use the white inverse mark on navy surfaces and the navy mark on light surfaces. Interactive property cards must keep their image, favorite, share, and media controls outside the body detail press target; do not nest pressable controls inside another pressable.

**Why:** On October 3, 2026, the user confirmed the roof/house + OG artwork as the official logo and requested its navy color to match the product theme; on October 9, 2026, they requested the double-ring splash with the “OG Landmark” name beneath, a 7-second minimum display, opposite ring motion, four larger dots per ring, and increasingly faster rotations. The three-pillar mark must remain removed throughout the app. The Expo Go loading view's label is the configured project name, not the in-app splash; changing it also changes the installed app's display name. The app is also rendered on web through React Native Web, where nested pressables become nested HTML buttons and produce hydration/runtime errors. Central tokens keep the visual language consistent across the many legacy screens.

**How to apply:** Extend `PolishedUI` and the semantic color/layout tokens before adding one-off controls. Use the official roof/house + OG mark in #0B1F3A on light surfaces and its white inverse on dark surfaces. Keep the three-pillar artwork out of active app, web, portal, legal-page, and icon assets. For app splash screens, use two subtle rings and small dots around the mark with “OG Landmark” below it. For cards, make the body the detail target and leave media/action controls as sibling press targets. Respect reduced-motion settings in shared animations.

Native status-bar icons do not support an arbitrary brand tint on iOS or Android; use the OS dark-content mode on a light status-bar area, and preserve intentional hiding in immersive full-screen media.

**Why:** The user requested navy system icons, but native status-bar APIs expose dark/light treatments rather than an exact custom icon color. Dark icons need a light area behind them to remain legible.

**How to apply:** Set the shared Expo status bar to dark content and Android's native status-bar style to `dark-content`. Do not draw substitute battery, signal, or time icons.
