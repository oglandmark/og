---
name: Play Store metadata claims
description: Avoiding a repeat Google Play Metadata policy rejection for OG Landmark.
---

Google Play rejected OG Landmark's English listing descriptions after they described the app as Pakistan's “trusted” real-estate platform and “Your Trusted Real Estate Platform.” Keep store copy factual about features and avoid unsupported trust, quality, ranking, awards, or popularity claims. Review store screenshots and promotional graphics for similar claims too.

**Why:** The October 7, 2026 Play Console notice cited the short and full description fields under Metadata policy.

**How to apply:** Before future Play submissions, review every listing locale and asset, use only claims supported by current app functionality, and submit the corrected store listing for review.
