Our Story

Built on Trust, Driven by Excellence

OG Landmark was founded in 2019 with a single mission: to make real estate transactions in Pakistan transparent, efficient, and trustworthy. We saw a market where buyers struggled to find verified properties, sellers had no professional platform, and agents lacked the tools to serve their clients properly.

Starting from Okara district — the heart of Punjab's agricultural belt — we built a platform that serves every type of property: from residential villas in DHA to fertile farmlands in Depalpur, from commercial plazas in Lahore to orchards in Renala Khurd.

Today, OG Landmark is a complete real estate ecosystem for buyers, property owners, agents, developers, and administrators. Our website and mobile app bring property discovery, listing submission, enquiries, saved searches, notifications, and practical land-calculation tools together in one place.
