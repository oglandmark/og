Account and data control

Delete your OG Landmark account

You can request deletion of your OG Landmark account and associated personal data even if you no longer have the app or cannot sign in.

Option 1: Delete from the app

  1. Open OG Landmark and sign in.
  2. Open Profile and select Delete Account.
  3. Review the confirmation and confirm the request.

Option 2: Request deletion on this page

Email support@oglandmark.com with the subject OG Landmark account deletion request. Send the request from the email address linked to your account, or include the account email address or mobile number so we can identify it. You do not need to reinstall the app.

Email a deletion request

What happens next

We may verify that you control the account before processing the request. The request covers account-related personal data, including profile information, saved app data, notification registration, and content associated with the account. Information may be retained where required by law or needed for security, fraud prevention, or an unresolved dispute; any retained information is limited to that purpose and kept only as long as needed.

Security: Never include your password or a one-time verification code in an email. If we need more information to verify a request, we will ask for it without asking for your password or OTP.

For details about information collected and how it is handled, read the OG Landmark Privacy Policy.
