Account and data control

Request deletion of OG Landmark data

You can ask OG Landmark to delete specific personal data associated with your account without requesting full account deletion. You can also request deletion of your account and its associated data.

Request deletion of specific data

  1. Email support@oglandmark.com with the subject OG Landmark data deletion request.
  2. Send the request from the email address linked to your account, or include the account email address or mobile number so we can identify it.
  3. Describe which information you want deleted. We may contact you to verify that you control the account before processing the request.
Email a data-deletion request

Request full account deletion

In the app, open Profile → Delete Account. If you cannot access the app, use the OG Landmark account-deletion page to submit a request by email.

What happens to a request

We review the request and may verify account ownership before taking action. Some information may need to be retained for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, or unresolved disputes. If information must be retained, its use is limited to that purpose and period.

Security: Do not send your password or a one-time verification code. We will not ask for either to process a deletion request.

For information about data handling and retention, read the OG Landmark Privacy Policy.
